# OPF 2026: source brief

Summarised from the organisers' documents, the *Ogun Poetry Festival 2026
Prospectus* and the *OPF 2026 Sponsorship & Partnership Proposal (revised)*.
The site's data lives in `src/data/festival.ts`; keep both in step.

## The festival

- **Name:** Ogun Poetry Festival (OPF). **Tagline:** Creativity for Humanity.
- **Founded:** 2023. Annual, multi-tiered cultural institution for literature,
  spoken word, civic leadership and youth creative enterprise.
- **2026 theme:** Artistic Expression for Advocacy.
- **When:** Saturday 5 December 2026, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
- **Where:** Cinema Hall, June 12 Cultural Centre, Abeokuta, Ogun State.
- **Audience:** a curated 200 to 250 student scholars, undergraduates,
  educators and cultural leaders.
- **Focus areas and SDGs:** mental health and emotional wellness (SDG 3),
  academic literacy (SDG 4), civic engagement and advocacy (SDG 16), creative
  entrepreneurship (SDG 8), cultural preservation (SDG 11).

## Track record

- 2023: over 100 secondary school students across 10+ public and private schools.
- Ongoing: 6+ recurring open mic and workshop sessions.
- 2025 flagship, "The Truth in Our Scars": over 200 youths, scholars,
  performers and creatives.
- Endorsed by the Ogun State Ministry of Culture and Tourism. Past keynote:
  Barrister Titilola Akinlawon (SAN).
- Corporate allies: ESET (Lead Grant Partner), Golden Penny Foods, Cliq, Kuta
  Art Gallery, Artvocacy, Artitude Hub.

## Competition (the OPF Rally)

From the *OPF 2026 School Entry Brochure*, which wins over the proposal where
they differ. The brochure is downloadable from the site.

| Track | Theme | Who | Format | Prizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Junior Secondary | Health & Mental Well-being | Max 2 writers per school, 20 schools | Written poetry, pre-screened by the academic review board; results confidential until the day | Cash 1st to 3rd; academic awards, hampers and certificates for top 3; game packs for top 5 schools |
| Senior Secondary | Health Advocacy (counterfeit drugs, adulterated food, public healthcare) | 1 video per school, exactly 5 seniors | Pre-recorded spoken word, theatre or protest piece, scored before the day | Top 3 awarded; game packs for top 5 schools |
| Tertiary Slam | Civic Engagement or The Creative Economy (one per institution) | 6 institutions: FCE, FUNAAB, Crescent, Chrisland, DBells, OGSCON (confirmed by the organisers) | Live performance only, judged on the day | 1st ₦90,000 + trophy; 2nd ₦60,000 + plaque. Fee ₦15,000 to ₦20,000 per institution |

The proposal's earlier wording ("Top 10 schools: Flow Game", "top 3
students: cash") is replaced by the brochure's.

**Delegation:** 20 secondary schools (10 public, 10 private), each bringing
exactly 20 students: 2 Junior writers, 5 Senior performers, 13 general
attendees for the workshops. Students also get an introduction to digital
literacy and AI-assisted storytelling; selected Junior entries may become an
AI-illustrated visual piece.

**Key dates:** September 2026 registration opens · mid-October 2026
registration closes · October to early December entries scored · 5 December
festival day.

Institutions enrol through the Secretariat; students, educators and
chaperones receive certificates; selected educators and chaperones receive
formal commendation.
